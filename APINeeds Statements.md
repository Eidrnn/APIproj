Reflection
Going into the partner interview, we expected the conversation to stay fairly high-level general budgeting needs, maybe a request for basic spending totals. What actually stood out was how quickly the discussion narrowed down to a single category: fitness. The partner was far more specific than anticipated about wanting to isolate fitness-related spending (gym memberships, equipment, classes) from the rest of their expenses, rather than just seeing it lumped into a generic "miscellaneous" bucket. This pushed our needs statements toward a full set scoped specifically to fitness expenses instead of a more generic expense-tracking API. It was also a useful reminder that "manage my spending" can mean very different things depending on what the user actually cares about tracking closely for this partner, fitness was clearly a priority area tied to a personal goal, not just another line item. That specificity will likely shape how we design the endpoints going forward, since a narrow, well-defined resource (fitness expenses) is easier to build correctly than a vague, catch-all one.
